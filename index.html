Autonomous Real-Time PPE Safety Compliance

SerBot is an autonomous mobile differential drive robotics platform engineered for factory and construction site safety audits. Operating on a two-wheel geared drivetrain with Cytron motor drivers and an elevated 45° downward-pitched camera mast, SerBot patrols hazardous work zones and identifies safety non-compliance in real time using YOLOv8 computer vision.

Model: SafetyVision v2 (YOLOv8s) Dataset: 80,000+ Industrial Images Drivetrain: 2WD Differential Drive Mast: 50cm Channel @ 45° Pitch Inference: Real-Time OpenCV
MECHANICAL ARCHITECTURE (CAD SCHEMATIC)
Ground Clearance: 4.5cm Pitch Angle: 45° Drive: Differential
⚙️ Hardware & Mechanical Engineering Specifications
Subsystem Breakdown
🏎️ Drivetrain
2-Wheel Differential Drive

High-traction rubber wheels driven by geared DC motors via Cytron DC driver for independent PWM speed and zero-radius turns.

🛡️ Power Isolation
Dual Isolated Switches

Separate master motor switch and isolated logic switch for ESP microcontroller to isolate inductive back-EMF spikes from resetting CPU.

📐 Sensor Mast
50 cm @ 45° Pitch

Elevated channel solves near-field blind spots, capturing complete head-to-torso profile of workers within 1–4 meters without tipping the robot.

🧠 Vision Pipeline
SafetyVision v2 (YOLOv8s)

Trained on 80,000+ industrial images. 13 classes including Hardhat, Safety Vest, Goggles, Gloves, Mask, plus negative violation classes.

🔄 End-to-End Real-Time Perception Pipeline
Execution Flow
STAGE 01
Optical Frame Capture
USB Camera captures 640×480 @ 30 FPS. Software performs 180° rotation to handle inverted hardware mounting used for clean cable routing.
→
STAGE 02
YOLOv8s Inference
Frame passes through anchor-free YOLOv8s detector at CONF=0.30 & IOU=0.45, generating coordinates, class indices, and confidence probabilities.
→
STAGE 03
Synonym & Violation Resolver
Synonym engine reconciles model class labels (e.g., "helmet" → "Hardhat") and identifies explicit negative labels (e.g. "NO-Hardhat").
→
STAGE 04
Compliance Evaluation & IoT
Calculates compliance %, renders on-screen HUD overlays, logs safety violation events, and structures JSON telemetry ready for cloud upload.
